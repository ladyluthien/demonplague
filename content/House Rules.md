This is a classic D&D adventure, and as such I'm keeping house rules simple. 
- Prestige will operate as written, with your home base being Tomar's Crossing. 
- You may attune to a number of magical items equal to your proficiency bonus.
- No guns/firearms in the setting.
- Not exactly a house rule, but: expect bringing the dead back to life to be very difficult. Anyone who could cast that kind of magic wouldn't live in a backwater like the Luna Valley was, and components of that value are vanishingly rare in a valley that's fighting over food.
