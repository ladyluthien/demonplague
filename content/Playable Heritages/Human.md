It is said that the ancient Raceon empire once settled in the Luna Valley. Their port city, Naradah - upon which the present-day city of Safeharbor was built - was a bustling city and hub of commerce. However, the Raceoni were betrayed by the druid circle of Minas Herion, who covered the valley in a glacier that has endured for the last thousand years. 

Human settlers of the Luna Valley have a special distaste for goblinoids, who have been raiding human settlements ever since the first pioneers arrived.
