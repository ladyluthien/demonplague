Luna Valley is far enough north that its climate has never been warm, but it is said that in millennia past, it was warmer than it is today. There are legends that the [[Yugtug]] hunted megafauna and the [[Raceon]] farmed the soil. However, for the past thousand years, the Luna Valley has been a frozen glacier, with only the most meager of settlements eking out an existence on the muddy rises that peek above the glacier. The winters are long, dark, and bitterly cold; in the height of summer, the darkest of night is still only twilight as the sun barely leaves the sky. The growing season is short, with implications for [[Trade, Food, and Dress]].
