4,000 Years Ago – The [[Duladarin]] elven civilization. Legend has it that something caused them to go mad and turn on each other. 
3,000 Years Ago – The [[Al Nasar]] dwarven civilization. The cause of their collapse is not known: they simply shuttered themselves in their great city of Maragath's Prize and never reopened the doors.
2,000 Years Ago – The [[Yugtug]] goblinoid civilization. They were destroyed by a cult known now only as the Cult of Chaos. 
1,000 Years Ago – The [[Raceon]] human empire. Their great city was Naradah, upon which present day Safeharbor was built. They were wiped out when the glacier came, covering the valley in a sheet of ice.
200 Years Ago – New groups of mixed humanoid races move to the frozen Luna Valley and form settlements.
