The dwarves of Al Nasar were a community of mountain dwarves. Amongst non-dwarves, all that is known is that they built a great city, Maragath's Prize, and shuttered themselves away in it. Amongst non-dwarves, that city has a dark reputation, and adventurers whisper that the treasure there is cursed. Dwarves, however, tell of even darker rumors - rumors they are reluctant to share with outsiders. 

Amongst dwarves, it is said that the dwarves of Al Nasar were fervently religious and sought to create the grandest dwarven civilization yet seen as a testament to Moradin. While competition is encouraged in the process of striving for ever greater accomplishments, the legends hold that the competition turned sour. Dwarves labored for weeks on end to create ever finer treasure, slowly going insane from the strain. The exact nature of this insanity varies, often depending on the moral that the tale's teller wishes to convey. The most common version, however, is that the dwarves in Maragath's Prize created items of such beauty that mortal minds could not comprehend. As a result, many contemporary dwarves, particularly in the Luna Valley, leave a deliberate flaw in their work to avoid such consequences and leave a door open for further creativity. 

Al Nasar dwarves worked in metal and gems, with particular skill in enameling. Stylistically, they favored symmetry and order - art deco meets brutalist meets Aztec. They built ziggurats as above-ground structures, often with many, many more levels below the relatively small above-ground structure. Early-stage Al Nasar craftsmanship is in bronze; as they mined deeper and improved in skill, they found ways of plating gold onto steel to make it strong enough for hard use. However, their most favored material was simple iron, and their wrought or cast iron pieces are near-impossible to damage or destroy.
