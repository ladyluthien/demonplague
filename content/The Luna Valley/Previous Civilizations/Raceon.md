The Raceon were a proud human civilization that settled the Luna Valley and were wiped out by the glacier that covers the valley in the present day. They sailed to it about a thousand years ago and found fertile farmland, untouched for centuries; it was quick work to build settlements, and the valley became a breadbasket which sustained the great port trading city of Naradah. 

Raceon civilization was wealthy, and as a result, the legends of that time are full of courtly manners, dashing adventurers, and swooning lovers (think Camelot, or Arabian Nights but colder). In Naradah, nobles schemed and plotted; out in the wilds, monster-hunters captured ferocious beasts for displays at tourneys. But then - the glacier came.

The exact circumstances of the catastrophe are somewhat unknown. Some claim that a group of rogue druids, angered by the civilization encroaching on their precious wilds, murdered all inhabitants, including themselves. Others claim that the glacier was a natural, or at least predictable, phenomenon, and the Naradah elite, made soft by their decadent lifestyle, were too weak to prevent it. Still others claim that it was not the Naradah's lifestyle but a plague that had rendered Naradah unable to respond, and write it off as a tragedy in many forms. 

Raceon art is very Saxon/Celt. Because they came from the sea originally, many designs favor fish and scale motifs. They favored colors and bold tapestries, some of which are still extant. Their city of Naradah was famed for its castles, upon the ruins of which present-day Safeharbor was built. While their weapons and armor were not as technically ornate as those of the [[Al Nasar]] and [[Duladarin]] civilizations, their elegant simplicity makes them prized heirlooms. Knights of Naradah would also paint their armor, reserving the more ornate designs for the painted designs rather than the actual shape of the armor. It was not uncommon for a Naradah knight to paint her lover's symbol on her breastplate, only to be jilted and turn up at the next tourney with a new symbol entirely.
