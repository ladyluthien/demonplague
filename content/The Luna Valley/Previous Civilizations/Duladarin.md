The Duladarin, also known as star elves, were a society of high elves that studied the stars. They were primarily devotees of [[Elven Pantheon|Letheri]]. It is said that they moved to what is now the Luna Valley after escaping some kind of persecution or horror; however, four thousand years of time have made the details vague even amongst the long-lived elven folk. (For context, in a real-world timeline, the Duladarin would have had the height of their civilization around the time that the Epic of Gilgamesh was written - by the *earliest* estimate.) 

What is known about the Duladarin is that they were great astrologers and druids, as well as skilled enchanters who blended craftsmanship with magic. They buried their dead in barrows guarded by constructs powered by magic, and the treasure of the star elves is the source of many an adventuring legend. Duladarin craftsmanship is distinguished by a fondness for working in stone and crystal first and mithral second. Stylistically, they favor the soft curves and organic shapes of many elven craftspeople, although their work is more stylized than later elven civilizations (think Hittite meets Art Nouveau).
