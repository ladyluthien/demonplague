Multiclass spells are from MOAR Complete and are specifically built for multiclass characters. They allow a character with spellcasting granted by one class to alter the key features granted to them by another class (such as Sneak Attack or Commanding Presence). Because of these considerations, multiclass spells are always 1st-level, yet many feature more powerful effects when cast with higher-level spell slots.

Multiclass spells always have the "Association" and "Prerequisite" lines in their writeup. To learn a multiclass spell, it must be on your spellcasting class spell list, you must have at least one character level in the associated class, and you must have the prerequisite class feature. Some archetypes make exceptions to this rule, granting special access to certain multiclass spells.

### Multiclass Spells (Adept)
###### Dance of War
1st-level (transmutation; arcane, enhancement, movement, multiclass)
Classes: Bard, sorcerer, witch
Association: Adept class
Prerequisite: Martial Arts class feature
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration (10 minutes)
You magically enhance your movement, becoming supernaturally graceful and agile. Until the spell ends, any time you make an Acrobatics check, Athletics check, Performance (dance) check, or Dexterity saving throw, roll your Martial Arts die and add it to the result.

###### Immovable Object
1st-level (transmutation; nature, protection, multiclass)
Classes: Druid, sorcerer, witch, wizard
Association: Adept class
Prerequisite: Martial Arts class feature
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration (10 minutes)
You root yourself in place with supernatural power. Until the spell ends, your speed becomes 0, and you automatically fail Dexterity saving throws. You gain resistance to all damage.
Also, at the start of each of your turns, roll your Martial Arts die. You gain a bonus to AC equal to the result until the beginning of your next turn.

###### Instant Recovery
1st-level (evocation; divine, healing, multiclass)
Classes: Cleric, druid, herald, witch
Association: Adept class
Prerequisite: Martial Arts class feature
Casting Time: 1 reaction, which you take when you take
damage of any kind
Range: Self
Components: V
Duration: Instantaneous
You respond to harm with an instinctive surge of healing energy. You reduce the triggering damage by an amount equal to your Martial Arts die + your Constitution modifier. If the damage prevented exceeds the damage dealt, you are healed for a number of hit points equal to the excess.
***Cast at Higher Levels.***
You roll an additional Martial Arts die for each slot level above 1st.

###### Planar Impact
1st-level (evocation; divine, attack, radiant, multiclass)
Classes: Cleric, herald, warlock, witch
Association: Adept class
Prerequisite: Martial Arts class feature
Casting Time: 1 reaction
Range: Self
Components: V
Duration: Concentration (1 minute)
Saving Throw: Constitution (special; see text)
You infuse your next strike with potent planar energies to punish your enemies and steal their sight. The next time you hit with a melee weapon attack while the spell is active, it deals additional radiant or necrotic damage (your choice) equal to your Martial Arts die. The target must also make a Constitution save against your spell save DC or be blinded until the end of your next turn. The spell then ends.
***Cast at Higher Levels.***
The spell deals an additional Martial Arts die of damage for each slot level above 1st.

##### Thrown Punches
1st-level (evocation; arcane, attack, force, unarmed, multiclass)
Classes: Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
Association: Adept class
Prerequisite: Martial Arts class feature
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Medium (60 feet)
Target: One creature within range
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration (1 minute)
You imbue your limbs with magic, allowing you to project the force of your attacks. When you cast this spell, make a ranged spell attack against a target within range. On a hit, you deal force damage equal to your Martial Arts die + your spellcasting ability modifier. Until the spell ends, you may substitute any unarmed strike you would make on your turn with the spell attack above.

### Multiclass Spells (Berserker)

###### Best Served Cold
1st-level (evocation; attack, arcane, cold, multiclass, weaponry)
Classes: Sorcerer, warlock, witch, wizard
Association: Berserker class
Prerequisites: Rage class feature
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration (1 minute)
Saving Throw: Constitution (special; see text)
You still your hot-blooded fury and strike your enemy with icy, malicious calm. The next time you hit with a melee weapon attack while the spell is active, it deals an additional 3d6 cold damage. The target must also make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or have its movement speed halved for 1 minute. The spell then ends. The creature may re-attempt the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. Additionally, if the creature takes any fire damage, the effect ends early.

You cannot cast this spell while raging even if you normally could due to archetype features or feat benefits. 

###### Infectious Fury
1st-level (enchantment; arcane, enhancement, multiclass)
Classes: Bard, druid, sorcerer, warlock, witch
Association: Berserker class
Prerequisites: Rage class feature
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Short(30 feet)
Target: One friendly creature within range
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 minute
You howl with fury, inviting your companions to join you in your wrath.
The next time you Rage while the spell is active, a willing creature within range joins you in the Rage, gaining the benefits and drawbacks of a 1st-level berserker’s Rage. Their Rage is  independent of yours and can end separately without affecting yours and vice versa.
Cast at Higher Levels.
You may choose an additional target for each slot level above 1st.

###### Irresistible Force
1st-level (enchantment; divine, enhancement, multiclass,
weaponry)
Classes: Bard, cleric, herald, warlock, witch
Association: Berserker class
Prerequisites: Rage class feature
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: 10 minutes
You draw upon magical energy to give yourself the capacity to smash through anything in your path.
The next time you Rage, you become even more dangerous and difficult to stop than normal. For the duration of that Rage, your weapon attacks' damage type changes to force damage, your movement speed can’t be reduced below 15 feet, and you ignore nonmagical difficult terrain. Also, if you would become stunned, you are instead rattled. When you stop raging, you suffer a level of fatigue and the spell ends.
Cast at Higher Levels.
If the spell level used to cast this is at least 2nd-level, if you would become frightened, you are instead rattled. If it is at least 3rd-level, if you would become paralyzed, you are instead slowed. If it is at least 4th-level, if you would become petrified, you are instead slowed. Durations are the
same, unless a condition would be permanent, in which case, it persists for one minute, then ends. This benefit applies even if the spell ends before the condition does.

###### Well of Wrath
1st-level (enchantment; arcane, enhancement, multiclass)
Classes: Bard, druid, sorcerer, warlock, witch
Association: Berserker class
Prerequisites: Rage class feature
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You magically funnel fury into yourself, pushing beyond your normal limits. To cast this spell, you must have no remaining uses of Rage, or there must be no limit to the number of times you can
Rage.
As part of the action to cast a spell, you enter a Rage, and you take 1d8 psychic damage which cannot be reduced or prevented in any way. While this Rage lasts, at the beginning of each of your turns you take another 1d4 psychic damage which cannot be reduced or prevented in any way. Also during this Rage, your melee weapon attacks deal an additional 1d4 psychic damage. When your Rage ends, you must make a Wisdom save against your own spell save DC or suffer a level of strife.
If you have unlimited Rage uses (such as in the case of a 20th level Wrathburner berserker), the bonus weapon damage is instead 2d8 psychic damage. You take no initial or ongoing damage and you have advantage on your saving throw against strife.

###### White-Hot Anger
1st-level (evocation; arcane, fire, multiclass, weaponry)
Classes: Bard, druid, sorcerer, warlock, witch
Association: Berserker class
Prerequisites: Rage class feature
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 minute
You magically stoke your anger to the intensity of a crucible. The next time you Rage while the spell is active, you glow from within from the heat of your fury. You shed bright light to a range of 10 feet and dim light a further 20 beyond that. Your melee weapon attacks deal an additional 1d6 fire damage, and any creature grappled by or grappling you at the start of its turn takes 1d6 fire damage.
Cast at Higher Levels.
The fire damage done by your melee weapon attacks and to creatures grappling or grappled by you increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.

###### Protective Striving
1st-level (abjuration; arcane, multiclass, protection)

Association: Fighter class
Prerequisites: Fighting Style class feature
Casting Time: 1 bonus action
Range: Self
Components: V, S

Until the spell ends, the first death saving throw you make after falling unconscious gains a bonus equal to your total exertion spent. If the result of this death save is 20 or more, treat the result as if you had rolled a 20.

### Multiclass Spells (Ranger)
###### Studied Defense
1 st-level (abjuration; divine, protection, multiclass)
Classes: Cleric, herald, witch, wizard
Association: Ranger class
Prerequisites: Studied Adversary class feature
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration (10 minutes)
You draw upon your research of a specific class of enemy and ward yourself against their attacks. Until the spell ends, you gain a +2 bonus to AC against attacks made by your Studied Adversaries. You also gain an expertise die on saving throws against effects your Studied Adversaries control.

###### Trailblaze
1st-level (divination; nature, movement, multiclass, protection)
Classes: Bard, druid, witch
Association: Ranger class
Prerequisites: Familiar Terrain class feature
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: 24 hours
You cut a path for others to follow, easing the journey for those who come after you. Until the spell ends, you leave a magically-cleared trail behind you. While creating the trail neither you nor your companions can use the Cover Tracks activity. The trail must follow a single route; if you backtrack or cross over the path cleared by this spell before the spell ends, its effect is suppressed until you resume travel from its farthest tip.
The magically cleared trail is 10 feet wide and made from packed soil, and it lasts indefinitely. Non-magical vegetation and hazards are cleared from the path. Subtle warding magic automatically disarms and dispels natural hazards with a save DC less than your spell save DC. Those who follow the trail cannot become lost except due to spell effects of 2nd-level and stronger. They also gain an expertise die on Survival checks made while journeying along the trail.

###### Studied Diplomacy
1st-level (enchantment; communication, divine, healing, multiclass)
Classes: Bard, herald, sorcerer, warlock, witch
Association: Ranger class
Prerequisites: Studied Adversary class feature
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: 1 hour
One of the best ways to rid yourself of enemies is to turn them into friends. Until the spell ends, you gain an expertise die on Culture and Persuasion checks involving your Studied Adversaries.
Additionally, when you restore hit points to a Studied Adversary, you may reroll any die result of 1 that comes up when determining the amount of hit points restored. You must use the new roll.

###### Quick Study
1st-level (divination, nature, knowledge, multiclass)
Classes: Bard, druid, witch, wizard
Association: Ranger class
Prerequisites: Studied Adversary class feature
Casting Time: 10 minutes
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You magically tap into the stored knowledge of scholars everywhere to gain the information you need to prevail against the foes you face.
You must cast this spell when you finish a short rest. You may immediately change your Studied Adversary choices as if you had just finished a long rest.

### Multiclass Spells (Rogue)
###### Igniting Strike
1st-level (evocation; attack, arcane, fire, multiclass, weaponry)
Classes: Sorcerer, wizard
Association: Rogue class
Prerequisites: Sneak Attack class feature
Casting Time: Special (see text)
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
Your weapons flashes with searing heat, setting your target ablaze. When you deal Sneak Attack damage with a melee attack, you may cast this spell as part of the Sneak Attack, transforming the bonus damage from your Sneak Attack into fire damage. You inflict ongoing fire damage to the
attack's target, which has a duration of 1 minute or until the creature uses an action to extinguish the flames. The amount of ongoing damage improves if cast at a higher spell-level:
1st-level: 1d6 of ongoing fire damage.
2nd-level: 2d6 of ongoing fire damage.
3rd-level: 3d6 of ongoing fire damage.
4th-level or higher: 4d6 of ongoing fire damage.
For the purposes of determining spells cast in a round, this spell counts as having a casting time of one action.

Prerequisites: Sneak Attack class feature
Casting Time: Special (see text)
Range: Self
Components: V, S

2nd-level: Blinded.
3rd-level: Blinded and rattled.
4th-level or higher: Blinded, rattled, and stunned. 

For the purposes of determining spells cast in a round, this spell counts as having a casting time of one action.

###### Inevitable Strike
1st-level (evocation; attack, arcane, force, multiclass, weaponry)
Classes: Sorcerer, wizard
Association: Rogue class
Prerequisites: Sneak Attack class feature
Casting Time: Special (see text)
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You pour raw magical energy into your attack, which hammers the target and spills over into the surrounding area. When you deal Sneak Attack damage with a melee attack, you may cast this spell as part of the Sneak Attack, transforming the bonus damage from your Sneak Attack into force damage. In addition, depending on the spell slot level used to cast this spell, one of the  following additional effects occur:
1st-level: The target must make a strength save against your spell save DC or fall prone.
2nd-level: The target must make a strength save against your spell save DC or fall prone, and a target within 10 feet (which may be the same target as the initial strike) takes 1d4+1 force damage.
3rd-level: the target must make a strength save against your spell save DC or fall prone, and two targets within 10 feet (either or both of which may be the same target as the initial strike) take 1d4+1 force damage.
4th-level or higher: The target must make a strength save against your spell save DC or fall prone, and three targets within 10 feet (any of which may be the same target as the initial strike) take 1d4+1 force damage.

For the purposes of determining spells cast in a round, this spell counts as having a casting time of one action.

Classes: Bard, warlock, witch
Association: Rogue class
Prerequisites: Sneak Attack class feature
Casting Time: Special (see text)
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous
You amplify your attack to wound not just the target’s body, but its mind as well. When you deal Sneak Attack damage with a melee attack, you may cast this spell as part of the Sneak Attack, transforming the bonus damage from your Sneak Attack into psychic damage. For the purposes of determining spells cast in a round, this spell counts as having a casting time of one action.
