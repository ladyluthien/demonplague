For millennia, people have settled in the Luna Valley, a great glacial valley far to the north. Elves, dwarves, goblinoids, and humans can all trace an ancestral claim to this region, but one catastrophe or another, coupled with the harsh weather of the valley, have ensured that as of present day, only a handful of settlements have been constructed on hills that rise above the glacier. 
At least, until the comet hit.
Two weeks ago, a firey comet fell to earth in the Luna Valley, setting off a glacial melt of apocalyptic proportions. Between heat, flooding, mudslides, and earth- and ice-quakes, all but one settlement has been destroyed. Tomar's Crossing is its name, and it is your last hope...

*The Demonplague* is a dark and semi-post-apocalyptic high magic hexcrawl-based campaign for levels 1-20. It is set entirely in the Luna Valley.
